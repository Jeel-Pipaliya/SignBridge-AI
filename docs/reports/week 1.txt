DAY 5 — Feature Extraction (63-Dimensional Invariant Vector)
🎯 Objective: Convert 21 landmarks into a 63-dimensional feature vector $[x_0, y_0, z_0, \dots, x_{20}, y_{20}, z_{20}]$ that is mathematically invariant to hand position and distance from camera.
🧠 What You Learned:
Translation Invariance: Centering coordinates by subtracting the wrist joint $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ so that the wrist is placed at $(0, 0, 0)$.
Scale Invariance: Dividing all centered coordinates by a reference distance (the Euclidean distance between the wrist and the middle finger MCP joint lm9). This removes sensitivity to how close or far the user's hand is from the lens.
